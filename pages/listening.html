TED Talk: The Power of Introverts

Susan Cain · Intermediate · 18:23

Transcript

When I was nine years old, I went off to summer camp for the first time. And my mother packed me a suitcase full of books, which to me seemed like a perfectly natural thing to do.

But the other kids in my cabin did not share my enthusiasm for reading. They wanted to put on plays and go swimming. And I just wanted to find a quiet corner to read my books.
